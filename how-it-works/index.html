How it works

A board, not a rumor mill

NEXTUP organizes a recruiting journey. The directory shows publicly listed 2027 ranking facts. Your dashboard and tracker stay yours and save on this device unless you connect your own Supabase project.

  1. 01

    Build the profile you control

    The dashboard is your workspace. Add a name, position, height, class, school, GPA if you want it public on your board, a short bio, and the season numbers you choose to enter. Completion is calculated from the fields that are filled.

  2. 02

    Keep film links with the profile

    Paste a YouTube link to embed it, or a Hudl link to open safely in a new tab. Removing a link updates the list immediately. Public prospect pages only embed a highlight when the ranking row published a stable URL.

  3. 03

    Track schools and your own dates

    Add a program, write notes, and move it among Interested, Contacted, and Evaluating. The contact date is the day you reached out. The follow-up date is the reminder you set. NEXTUP does not contact coaches and does not list offers.

Try the demo

No account is required. Sign in with a demo name if you want the welcome line personalized. Checklist marks, profile edits, film links, saved players, and school notes persist in local storage.
