Personal profile

Build the file. Run the process.

College recruiting gets clearer when sport, class, measurables, academics, and film live in one place. Then you name the schools you want and record the outreach you send. NEXTUP keeps that work on your board. It does not contact coaches or assign someone to recruit for you.

  1. Build one profileSport, class, height, and the academics you choose to share, in a single file.
  2. Add your filmKeep a YouTube or Hudl link you control next to those facts.
  3. List target schoolsName the programs you are researching and sort them as the list changes.
  4. Track outreachLog the day you reached out and the follow-up you set. You send every message.
